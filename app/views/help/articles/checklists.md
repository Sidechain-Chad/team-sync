## Add a checklist

Open the card and click **Checklist** in the quick-add row near the title. Give it a name and save. A card can hold more than one checklist; there's no limit on how many.

## Add items

Each checklist has its own **Add item** field at the bottom. Type an item and press Enter to add it and immediately start the next one.

## Check items off

Click the checkbox next to any item. The checklist's progress bar and percentage update immediately.

## Rename a checklist

You can't. Checklists have no rename control once created, so if the name needs to change, delete it and add a new one with the items re-entered.

## Delete a checklist

Click **Delete** next to the checklist's name. This removes every item in it. There's no confirmation step and no undo.

Checklist progress also shows on the card's board tile, as a small `x/y` count, so you can see how far along a card is without opening it.
