## Add an attachment

Open the card and click **Attachment** in the quick-add row. Choose a file. Images, PDFs, and most common document and media formats are accepted, up to a size cap enforced on upload.

Each attachment appears as its own row: filename, how long ago it was added, a **Download** link, and a **Delete** link.

## Card covers

The first image you attach to a card becomes its cover automatically: a thumbnail shown on the board tile above the title. This isn't a separate step or a setting. Attach a different image first, or delete the current cover attachment, and the next image in line takes over.

Non-image attachments, like PDFs, never become a cover, even if they're the first thing attached.

## Remove an attachment

Click **Delete** on its row inside the card. If it was the card's cover, the tile falls back to plain text, or to the next remaining image attachment.
