## Search

Click the search field in the top bar, or press `/` from anywhere on a board. With nothing typed, it shows your recently viewed boards. Type a query and it searches board names and card titles, descriptions, and comments together, showing boards and cards as two separate groups. Closed boards, and cards on them, are left out of results.

## Filter a board

Click **Filter cards** in a board's header, or press `f`. Filters combine across three groups: due-date status, labels, and members, including an **Unassigned** option for cards with nobody on them. Checking more than one option within a group is an OR; checking options across groups narrows further. Filtering only changes what's visible on screen. It doesn't change the URL into something permanent, and it resets when you leave the board.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Press `?` on a board to see the full list. The ones worth knowing without looking it up:

- `/` focuses search.
- `f` opens the filter popover.
- Arrow keys move a focus ring between cards, within a list or across to the next one.
- `Enter` opens the focused card.
- `n` adds a card to the focused list.
- `c` archives the focused card.
- `Esc` closes whichever is open on top: the shortcuts overlay first, then the filter popover, then the card modal.

Shortcuts only work on the board screen. They're not active on the planner, the map, or account pages.
