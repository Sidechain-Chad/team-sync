## Add a member to a board

Open the board menu, choose **Edit board details**, and scroll to **Team Members**. Type an email and click **Invite**.

This only works if that email already belongs to a TeamSync account. There's no invitation flow: nobody receives a signup link, and the person isn't notified that they've been added. If the email doesn't match an existing account, TeamSync tells you it couldn't find one. Share access another way first if the person you want doesn't have an account yet.

## Remove a member from a board

On the same **Team Members** list, click **Remove** next to their name. Removing someone from a board also removes them from every card on it; it doesn't unassign them card by card first.

## Add a member to a card

Open the card and, under **Members**, click the **+** button. The picker splits into **Card members** (already on this card) and **Board members** (everyone else on the board who isn't yet). Click a board member to add them.

## Remove a member from a card

Click their avatar under **Members** on the open card, or use the same picker. Either way, this only removes them from the card, not from the board.

Board membership and card membership are two different lists. Being on the board doesn't put you on every card, and being on a card doesn't require any special role beyond ordinary board membership. Every member of a board has the same permissions on it; only the board's creator is treated differently, and only for closing, reopening, deleting the board, and managing who's on it.
