Every new board starts with ten labels, one per color, unnamed until you rename them. Labels belong to a board, not to a single card, so renaming one changes it everywhere it's used.

## Add a label to a card

Open the card, and under **Labels** click the **+** button. Pick any existing label from the list to attach it. Click it again to remove it.

## Rename a label or give it a color name

In the same label picker, click the pencil next to a label to edit its name and color. Unnamed labels still work as filters and still show as colored pills; naming them just makes them easier to tell apart at a glance.

## Create a new label

At the bottom of the label picker, click **Create a new label**. Give it a name and pick a color from the fixed palette.

## Delete a label

Open the label's edit form from the picker and use **Delete** there. This removes it from every card that had it, not just the one you were looking at.

A card can carry as many labels as you attach. There's no limit, though a card with a dozen label pills stops being scannable, so most boards settle on a handful of meanings per label and reuse them consistently.
