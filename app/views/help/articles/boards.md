## Create a board

Click **New Board**, name it, and click **Create board**. Two fields on this form are optional: a board logo (a small image shown on the board tile and in the sidebar, not the large background behind your lists) and a list of emails to invite, separated by commas.

Inviting at creation time has a real limit: it only works for people who already have a TeamSync account under that exact email address. An email that doesn't match an existing account is silently ignored, with no error and no invitation sent. See [Members on cards and boards](/help/members) for the same limit on the more visible "Invite" flow.

## Set a background

Open the board, click its name to open the board menu, and choose **Edit board details**. Under **Board background**, choose a file and save. There's a **Remove current background** checkbox if you want to clear it. Until you set one, TeamSync fills the space with a generated gradient, deterministic per board rather than random, so the same board always gets the same one.

## Star a board

Click the star icon on a board tile, or on the board itself, to add it to your **Starred** section on the workspace page. Click it again to remove it. Starring is personal: it doesn't change what anyone else sees.

## Close and reopen a board

Open the board menu and choose **Close board**. Closed boards drop out of your workspace page and out of search, and move to **Closed boards** in the sidebar. Reopening is owner-only: click **Reopen** next to the board there.

Closing a board hides it. It does not lock it. Anyone who still has the URL to a card or list on a closed board can still open and edit it directly. Treat closing as tidying your workspace, not as an archive with restricted access.

## Delete a board

Open the board menu and choose **Delete board**, or use the **Delete board** link on the Edit page. This is permanent and only available to the board's owner.
