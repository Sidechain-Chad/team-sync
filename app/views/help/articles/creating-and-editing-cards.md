## Create a card

Click **Add a card** at the bottom of a list, type a title, and press Enter. It lands at the bottom of that list, ready to open.

This is the densest screen in TeamSync: members, labels, dates, description, checklists, attachments, location, and comments all live here. The rest of the articles in this Cards section cover each piece.

## Rename a card

You can rename a card two ways, without opening it fully:

- Hover a card tile on the board and click the pencil icon that appears.
- Inside the open card, click the title itself, next to the completion circle.

Both swap the title into an editable field in place. Edit it and press Enter, or click away.

## Write a description

Open the card and click **Edit** next to **Description**. The editor supports headings, bold and italic, bullet and numbered lists, links, images, code blocks, and dividers, using its own toolbar rather than markdown you type by hand. Click away or use the editor's own save action to keep your changes.

## Mark a card complete

Click the circle to the left of the card's title, either on the board tile or inside the open card. This is separate from moving a card to a "Done"-style list: completion is its own field, and TeamSync doesn't infer it from which list a card sits in.

## The `⋯` menu

Inside an open card, the `⋯` button next to the watch icon opens **Copy Card** and **Archive Card**. Moving a card to another list, and watching it, are separate controls next to `⋯`, not inside it. See [Copying and archiving](/help/copying-and-archiving) and [Watching and notifications](/help/watching-and-notifications).
