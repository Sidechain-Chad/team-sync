Both views pull cards from every board you can see, not just one, and both are scoped to a single month at a time.

## The planner calendar

Open **Planner** from the sidebar. It shows a standard month grid, with each card that has a due date or a start date appearing on the relevant day. A card with both a start and a due date renders as one continuous bar spanning the days between, labeled with the board it belongs to, rather than as a separate chip on every day it covers.

Use **Today** to jump back to the current month, and the arrows beside it to step forward or back a month at a time.

## The map

Click **Map** from the same header to switch from the calendar grid to a map, still scoped to the same month. It plots every card in that month that has a location set, across all your boards, with each marker labeled by board name and due date.

A board can also show its own map, scoped to just that board and not to a month: open the board and click **Map** in the bottom bar. Cards without a due date still show up there, since it isn't filtered by month.

## No located or dated cards

If nothing in the current month has a location, the map shows an empty state instead of a blank canvas. The calendar still renders its grid either way; an empty month just has no chips in it.
