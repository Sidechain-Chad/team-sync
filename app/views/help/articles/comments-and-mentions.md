## Write a comment

Open a card and use the **Write a comment...** field on the right. Type your comment and send it. It appears immediately, newest at the top, mixed in with the card's activity log: card moves, completed checklist items, due-date changes, and the like all show in the same feed.

## Mention someone

Type `@` followed by a few letters of a board member's name, and a list of matches appears below the field. Click a name to insert it. The mention becomes plain text in the comment, exactly as typed; it isn't a special link and doesn't highlight when the comment is later shown, but it does trigger a notification to that person.

Only board members show up in the mention list. You can't mention someone who isn't on the board the card belongs to.

## Delete a comment

You can delete your own comments. Look for **Delete** on a comment you wrote; it isn't available on anyone else's.

## Hide the feed

Click **Hide details** above the comment field to collapse the whole comments-and-activity column, if the card's other fields need the room. Click it again to bring the feed back.
