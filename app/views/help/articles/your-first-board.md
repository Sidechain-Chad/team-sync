TeamSync organizes work into three layers: boards, lists, and cards. A board is a project or a workspace. Lists are the stages work moves through. Cards are the individual tasks.

## Create a board

Click **New Board** on the workspace page. Give it a name. You can optionally add a logo and invite people by email at this point, or skip both and do it later.

TeamSync creates three lists automatically: To Do, Doing, and Done. It also seeds ten default labels in a range of colors, ready to rename.

## Add a list

Scroll to the end of your board's lists and click **Add another list**. Type a name and press Enter. There's no limit to how many lists a board can have.

## Add a card

Click **Add a card** at the bottom of any list. Type a title and press Enter, or click away to cancel. The card appears at the bottom of the list, ready to open.

## Open a card

Click anywhere on a card except its title or the small controls at the edges. This opens the card modal: the full detail view where you set dates, add labels, write a description, and everything else. See [Creating and editing cards](/help/creating-and-editing-cards) for what's in there.

## Move things around

Drag a card between lists, or drag a list to reorder it against its neighbors. Both use your mouse; there's no keyboard equivalent for dragging itself, though [keyboard shortcuts](/help/finding-things) exist for opening and archiving a focused card.

That's the whole loop: boards hold lists, lists hold cards, and dragging is how work moves. Everything else in TeamSync builds on top of it.
