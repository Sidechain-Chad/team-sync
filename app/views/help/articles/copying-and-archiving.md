title: Copying and archiving
category: Cards
position: 6
---

## Copy a card

Open the card, click `⋯`, and choose **Copy Card**. Name it and pick a destination list, on the same board or a different one. Labels and members come with it. Checklists come too, but reset to incomplete. Comments and activity history don't copy.

## Copy a list

From a list's **List actions** menu, choose **Copy list**. It copies every active card in the list to the end of the same board, same rules as copying a single card: labels, members, and reset checklists come along, comments and archived cards don't.

## Copy a board

Open the board menu and choose **Copy board**. TeamSync copies the board's lists, active cards, labels, and members, along with its logo and background. Archived cards, favorites, closed state, comments, and activity history are left behind. Anyone who can already see the board can copy it, not only its owner.

## Archive a card

Open the card, click `⋯`, and choose **Archive Card**. An archived card disappears from its list and from the board, but the record isn't deleted.

## Restore an archived card

Open the board menu and choose **Archived items**. Find the card and click **Restore**. It goes back to its original list.

## Permanently delete an archived card

On the same **Archived items** page, click **Delete** on a card's row. Unlike archiving, this can't be undone.

Archiving and deleting are two different actions with two different consequences. Archive first if you're not certain; delete only when you are.
