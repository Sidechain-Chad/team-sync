## Add a list

Click **Add another list** at the end of a board's lists, type a name, and press Enter.

## Rename a list

Click the list's name. It turns into a text field in place. Edit it and press Enter, or click away, and it saves automatically.

## Reorder lists

Drag a list by its header to move it left or right against the others.

## Sort cards within a list

Open the list's **List actions** menu (the `⋯` icon) and pick a sort under **Sort by**: due date, title A to Z, or newest first. This reorders the cards once. It isn't a live mode: dragging a card afterward, or adding a new one, doesn't re-sort anything.

## Set a work-in-progress limit

In the same **List actions** menu, set a number under **Work-in-progress limit**. Leave it blank for no limit. Going over it shows a warning badge on the list header, like `4/3`, but it never blocks you from adding another card. Treat it as a signal to yourself, not a rule TeamSync enforces.

## Archive every card in a list

Also in **List actions**: **Archive all cards**. This archives every active card in the list in one action. It doesn't touch cards already archived, and it doesn't delete the list itself.

## Delete a list

Click the trash icon on the list header. This is permanent for the list. Its cards go with it, so archive or move anything you want to keep first.

## Copy a list

From **List actions**, choose **Copy list**. TeamSync copies the list's active cards to the end of the same board, along with their labels, members, and checklists (reset to incomplete). Archived cards, comments, and activity history don't come along. See [Copying and archiving](/help/copying-and-archiving) for the board- and card-level equivalents.
